Privacy Policy — TMI 60초 Publisher

Last updated: 2026-10-18

TMI 60초 Publisher ("the App") is a private publishing tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @tmi60sec. It posts short educational videos that the account owner has produced and explicitly approved to that same owner's TikTok account. The App has exactly one user: the account owner.

Data we collect

  • TikTok OAuth credentials. When the account owner authorizes the App via TikTok Login Kit, TikTok issues an access token and refresh token. These tokens are stored on the owner's private server and used only to call TikTok's APIs on the owner's behalf.
  • TikTok creator profile information (user.info.basic scope): the account's open ID, display name, and avatar. Used only to display which account a video will be posted to before publishing, so the owner can confirm the correct account.
  • Video content the owner provides (video.upload / video.publish scopes): video files, captions, and hashtags the owner has approved for posting. Videos are transmitted to TikTok's servers solely for publishing.

Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect data about any other TikTok user. We do not collect viewers, followers, comments, or analytics. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers. There is no public-facing website and no other end user.

How data is used

Data is used for a single purpose: publishing owner-approved videos to the owner's own TikTok account (@tmi60sec) via TikTok's Content Posting API. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. The only external recipient of data is TikTok itself, when the owner publishes a video through TikTok's official APIs.

Data retention and deletion

OAuth tokens are kept only while the App is in use and are deleted when the owner revokes authorization (in TikTok's authorized-apps settings) or asks for deletion. Published videos remain on TikTok under TikTok's own policies; the owner can delete them in the TikTok app at any time.

Security

Tokens are stored server-side on the owner's private infrastructure and are never embedded in client-side code or public repositories.

Contact

Questions about this policy: the account owner via TikTok direct message to @tmi60sec.
